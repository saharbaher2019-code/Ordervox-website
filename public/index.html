FAQ

Good questions

Does it replace my staff?

No — it handles the phone so your staff can focus on the kitchen and the dining room. Anything it can't confidently handle gets warmly transferred to a real person.

What if it mishears an order?

Every item is read back and confirmed before the order is placed, and the customer gets a full read-back with the total before anything is submitted.

Can I keep my existing phone number?

Yes — OrderVox works with the number you already have. Customers dial the exact same number they always have.

How long does setup take?

Most restaurants are live within an afternoon — no new hardware, no rewiring your phone line.

Can I edit my menu?

Yes, anytime. Update items, prices, and modifiers, and OrderVox reflects the change on the very next call.

Can it transfer calls to a real person?

Absolutely — anything it can't confidently handle gets a warm handoff to your team.

Does it speak multiple languages?

Yes — English, Spanish, and Dari today, detected automatically mid-call, with more on the way.

Does it work after hours?

Yes — it's on 24/7, so you capture orders and questions even after the doors are closed.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — no long-term contract. Cancel whenever you like.

Is OrderVox an AI phone answering service for restaurants?

Yes — OrderVox is an AI voice agent built specifically for restaurants. It answers your phone like a trained host, takes orders accurately, and hands off to a real person for anything it can't confidently handle.

How is this different from voicemail or a phone tree?

Voicemail and "press 1" menus lose customers — most people hang up rather than leave a message or navigate a menu. OrderVox has a real conversation, takes the order, and confirms it, the way a person would.

Is my customers' data secure?

Yes — all customer data is encrypted, and OrderVox never sells or shares call recordings or customer information with third parties.

I'm not very tech-savvy — is this hard to manage?

No — there's no dashboard you're required to babysit. Orders arrive as a notification with an audio alert, the same way a text message would. We handle setup for you, not the other way around.

Does it work with my POS system?

Direct POS and delivery-platform integrations (Toast, Square, Clover, DoorDash, Uber Eats) are on our roadmap, not live yet. Today, orders land on your OrderVox dashboard and notify your team directly — no POS connection required to get started.
