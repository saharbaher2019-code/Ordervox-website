Overview

This policy covers this website (ordervox.net) and the demo-request form on it. It does not cover how the OrderVox phone-ordering product itself handles a restaurant's caller data (recordings, transcripts, order history) — that's addressed in a separate agreement with each restaurant customer, which should also be reviewed by an attorney before real customers rely on it.

Information we collect

  • What you submit through the demo-request form: your name, restaurant name, phone number, email address, and preferred language.
  • Standard technical information collected automatically by most websites, such as browser type and IP address, for security and troubleshooting.

This site does not currently use analytics or advertising tracking cookies.

How we use it

  • To respond to your demo request and get in touch about scheduling.
  • To send occasional updates about OrderVox, which you can opt out of at any time.

We do not sell your information to third parties.

Text messaging (SMS)

If you provide a phone number while placing an order through OrderVox's AI phone agent on behalf of a restaurant, you may receive a one-time SMS order confirmation and, if applicable, a follow-up "your order is ready" text for that specific order. Message frequency depends on how many orders you place — typically one to two messages per order.

Mobile phone numbers collected for order-confirmation texts are not shared or sold to third parties for marketing purposes.

Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out of text messages, or HELP for assistance.

Data retention and security

Demo-request submissions are stored securely and kept only as long as reasonably needed to follow up with you and maintain business records. Reasonable technical measures are used to protect this data, though no system is 100% secure.
